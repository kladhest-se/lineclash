# Game modes

## Multiplayer rooms

Up to eight participants can share a room. Room rules control hold, attacks, garbage targeting, speed-up and whether the last player wins. The host can configure the room before the round; server operators can restrict available features.

## Practice with bots

Play alone or add easy, medium or hard AI opponents through the room’s AI controls. Bot and solo rounds are casual, making them useful for practicing placement and specials without affecting ranked wins.

## Ranked and casual

A ranked round requires at least two humans, no active bots, and clients using the server-processed input protocol. The server simulates piece movement, gravity, holds, locks and clears, and derives awards from those results. Legacy board-reporting clients remain playable, but their rounds are casual. Stopped rounds are casual too.

Ranked results still depend on trusting the server operator. A legitimate protocol does not prevent account sharing, collusion or an operator fabricating reports. Casual rounds remain in match history.

## Team play

Players on the same named team cooperate. Set or clear your team in the lobby. Team and individual results are displayed separately where supported by the server.

## Spectating

Join as a spectator to watch without occupying a playing board, or continue watching after elimination. Spectators do not send gameplay inputs or earn a ranked win by observing.
