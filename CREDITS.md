Music was obtained from the linked OpenGameArt submissions on 13 November 2026. The cyberpunk-pack pieces share Alexander Ehlers / tricksntraps attribution and CC0; each piece is listed individually. Sky Portal is included once despite appearing twice in the requested list. Blue Space uses the author’s v0.96 WAV. Unchained Destiny uses both original WAV sections without editing their bytes. Cataloged download URLs, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in [music/tracks.json](music/tracks.json).

CC-BY licenses require attribution; their linked license pages define the terms. Credits and license links accompany the music in the player, About, this file and packaged distributions. No endorsement by the artists is implied.

## Audio decoder components

- **dr_mp3 and dr_wav**, David Reid / [dr_libs](https://github.com/mackron/dr_libs): bundled with their upstream public-domain/MIT license alternatives and notices.
- **stb_vorbis**, Sean Barrett and contributors / [stb](https://github.com/nothings/stb): bundled with its upstream public-domain/MIT license alternatives and notices.

Decoder notices are retained in clients/gui/third_party/. Exact downloaded-source hashes are in its SOURCES.json. The original MOD player remains in the source for compatibility history; the bundled soundtrack uses the streaming MP3/WAV/Ogg engine.

[Bundled decoder license notices](music/DECODER-LICENSES.txt) are also included in native distributions.

## Interface typography

Roboto by the Roboto Project Authors is used for the antialiased GUI font atlas under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See [the bundled license](docs/licenses/Roboto-OFL.txt). Font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/roboto. The atlas is generated by `scripts/build_ui_font.py`; no font library is needed at runtime.
